Read faster.
Actually remember it.

ReadRush shows one word at a time at the pace you choose, holds a red focus letter still in the centre, then asks comprehension questions — so your score rewards understanding, not just flicking through words.

One word, one place

Rapid Serial Visual Presentation keeps every word centred, so your eyes stop travelling back and forth across the line.

Effective WPM

Speed multiplied by comprehension. 800 WPM at 90% understanding scores 720 — flicking through words earns you nothing.

Set the pace per challenge or save a preferred speed. Pause, resume and restart whenever you need to.

A daily challenge, consecutive-day streaks and a personal best, so practice has something to push against.
