Dwello

by AirTrace

A little desk robot that senses your room with radar — not a camera — and actually reacts to you. Alexa waits for you to speak. Dwello already knows you walked in.

  • Senses the room — presence, movement and position, even when you sit still
  • No camera. Ever. — no lens, no video, nothing leaves your room
  • Has a face — blinks, looks around, sleeps when you go, wakes when you're back
  • Talks back — greets you, answers you, tells you to stand up once in a while
  • Runs your room — walk in, lights on; leave, everything powers down

Ptrionix

ptrionix.com

Every project. One blueprint. Ptrionix is the operating system for construction and interior design firms — auto-generate BOQs, track materials, finance and site logs, and run every project from one AI-powered plan instead of six spreadsheets.

  • AI-generated bills of quantity straight from the drawing set
  • Material tracking from purchase order to site delivery
  • Project finance — budget, spend and margin in one ledger
  • Daily site logs, vendor records and team assignments
  • Built with the trades who actually run the sites

Beamfall

iPhone & Android · Puzzle

A beam-routing puzzle. Fire a beam across a grid, rotate mirrors and prisms to bend it, and light every target in its own colour. 100 levels, every tenth one a boss run — and every level proven solvable by computer before it shipped.

  • 100 hand-verified levels, 10 boss runs
  • No advertising and nothing to buy — ever
  • No account, no sign-in, no data collected
  • Works fully offline, in aeroplane mode
