Beamfall — Account deletion

Delete your Beamfall account

Beamfall is published by PTRI Innovation Pvt. Ltd. You can delete your account and every score attached to it from inside the app, in about ten seconds, with no email and no waiting.

Beamfall iOS & Android Deletion is immediate

From inside the app

This is the fastest route and it deletes everything immediately.

  1. Open Beamfall.
  2. On the title screen, tap LEADERBOARD.
  3. Make sure you are signed in — the screen shows SIGNED IN AS and your name.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete. Your account and all scores are removed straight away.

By email

If you have lost access to the device or cannot sign in, email info@ptriinnovation.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject Beamfall account deletion. We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days, and in practice far sooner.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your account Deleted permanently. The sign-in record is removed, along with the email address associated with it.
Display name Deleted permanently. Your entry disappears from the global leaderboard immediately.
Scores and level results Deleted permanently. Every level result — taps, diamonds, whether a hint was used — is removed.
Notification token Deleted permanently. We can no longer send you notifications.
Your progress on your own device Kept. It never leaves your phone unless you sign in, so deleting the account does not cost you your levels. You can carry on playing exactly as before.

Nothing is retained after deletion beyond what the law requires of us, and there is no backup copy held for a retention period. We do not sell or share any of this data with third parties, and we never have.

You may not need an account at all

Beamfall is complete without one. All 100 levels, all three hints, and every setting work with no account and no sign-in. An account exists only so your name can appear on the global leaderboard and so your progress can follow you to another device. If you never sign in, nothing about you is transmitted or stored anywhere.

Full detail is in our privacy policy, and other questions are answered on the support page.
