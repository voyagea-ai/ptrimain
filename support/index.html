Beamfall

Fire a beam across a grid, rotate mirrors and prisms to bend it, and light every target in its own colour. Tap a piece to turn it — that is the only control in the game.

100 levels 10 boss runs no ads no purchases plays offline
EmitterFires the beam. Fixed in place — you cannot turn it.
TargetMust be lit in its own colour. Light them all to clear the level.
MirrorTap to flip the diagonal. Bounces light through 90°.
SplitterBounces light and lets it carry straight on through the gap.
PrismA corner channel. Light entering the wrong face is absorbed.
TintColoured glass. Recolours any beam passing straight through it.
GateA colour lock. Only its own colour gets past.
WallBlocks everything. Route around it.
Beamfall — Questions
What does “par” mean?

Par is the fewest taps a level can possibly be solved in — and it is not an estimate. Before the game shipped, every level was solved exhaustively by computer, testing every possible combination of piece orientations. Par is the proven minimum. Match it and you earn three diamonds.

What makes a boss level different?

Every tenth level is a boss: a larger grid, more pieces, and often a second emitter firing a second beam. On the later bosses a containment rule also applies — no beam may escape the board at all, which is shown as “NO LEAKS” under the level name.

I am completely stuck. Is the level definitely solvable?

Yes. Every level is guaranteed solvable from exactly the position it starts in — this is verified automatically for all 100 levels before each release, so you are never facing an impossible board. Tap ? in-game for a reminder of what each piece does, or ↺ to reset the level and try a different route.

Is there a timer, lives, or energy?

None of those. There is no timer, no lives system, no energy meter and nothing that stops you playing or asks you to wait. Take as long as you like.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device and survives app updates. Because there is no account system — by design — it does not transfer between devices, and deleting the app deletes it permanently.

Does Beamfall need an internet connection?

Never. All 100 levels ship inside the app and it works in aeroplane mode. Beamfall makes no network requests of any kind — see our privacy policy for the full detail.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No, and there is no plan to add them. The game is free, complete, and has nothing to sell you.

How do I report a bug or a broken level?

Email info@ptriinnovation.com. If it is about a specific level, please include the level number and, if you can, a screenshot — it makes the problem much faster to find.

Still stuck?

Write to us and a person will read it. Include the product name and, if something broke, what you were doing at the time.
