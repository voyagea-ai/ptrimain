Beamfall

Fire a beam across a grid, rotate mirrors and prisms to bend it, and light every target in its own colour. Tap a piece to turn it — that is the only control in the game.

Beamfall — Questions
What does “par” mean?

Par is the fewest taps a level can possibly be solved in — and it is not an estimate. Before the game shipped, every level was solved exhaustively by computer, testing every combination of piece orientations. Par is the proven minimum. Match it and you earn three diamonds.

What makes a boss level different?

Every tenth level is a boss: a larger grid, more pieces, and often a second emitter firing a second beam. On the later bosses a containment rule also applies — no beam may escape the board at all, shown as “NO LEAKS” under the level name.

I am completely stuck. Is the level definitely solvable?

Yes. Every level is guaranteed solvable from exactly the position it starts in — this is verified automatically for all 100 levels before each release, so you are never facing an impossible board. Tap ? in-game for a reminder of what each piece does, or ↺ to reset the level and try a different route.

Do I need an account?

No. Every level, every hint and all your progress work with no account at all, entirely offline. Signing in with Apple is optional and only adds your name to the global leaderboard. If you never sign in, the app sends nothing anywhere.

How do I delete my leaderboard account?

Open the leaderboard in the app and tap Delete account. That permanently removes your account, your display name and every score attached to it. Your progress on your own device is kept, so you can carry on playing. You can also email us and we will do it for you. Full step-by-step instructions, and exactly what is deleted, are on our account deletion page.

How does the leaderboard rank players?

By diamonds earned, with fewest total taps breaking ties — so efficiency wins over volume. Because every level’s par is a proven minimum number of moves, a score below it is mathematically impossible and the server rejects it outright.

How many hints do I get?

Three, for the whole game — not three per level. Spend them where they matter. A hint points at exactly the piece worth turning next and never leads to a dead end. Using one caps that level at two diamonds, so a perfect score still means you found the shortest route yourself.

Is it really free? What is the catch?

There is no catch. Beamfall is completely free, with no advertising, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no “premium” levels and nothing at all to pay for. All 100 levels are included from the moment you install it. We built it because we wanted to build it.

Is there a timer, lives, or energy?

None of those. No timer, no lives system, no energy meter and nothing that stops you playing or asks you to wait. Take as long as you like.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device and survives app updates. Deleting the app deletes it. If you sign in for the leaderboard, your progress is also saved to your account and comes back when you sign in on another device — but signing in is entirely optional, and without it your progress stays on that one device.

Does Beamfall need an internet connection?

No. All 100 levels ship inside the app and every one of them is playable in aeroplane mode. The only part that uses the network is the optional leaderboard — if you never sign in, Beamfall makes no network requests at all. See our privacy policy for the full detail.

How do I report a bug or a broken level?

Email info@ptriinnovation.com. If it is about a specific level, please include the level number and, if you can, a screenshot — it makes the problem much faster to find.

Still stuck?

Write to us and a person will read it. Include the product name and, if something broke, what you were doing at the time.
