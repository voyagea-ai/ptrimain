This website

ptriinnovation.com is a static site. It has no user accounts, no database and no server-side logic of its own. In practice that means:

  • No cookies are set and nothing is written to your browser's local storage.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts run. We do not measure your visit, and we cannot tell you apart from any other visitor.
  • Our contact form does not submit anywhere. It assembles a message and hands it to your own email application. Nothing reaches us until you press send yourself.

Two things that are honest to disclose

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore makes a request to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request, as it would for any site using the service.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested file) for security and reliability, as essentially all web hosting does. We do not analyse those logs to build any profile of you. If you email us, we hold that email in our mailbox so we can reply.

Beamfall for iPhone

Play without an account — nothing is sent

The game collects nothing unless you sign in.

All 100 levels, your progress, your hints and every setting work entirely on your device, offline, with no account. Used that way Beamfall makes no network requests at all and we receive nothing whatsoever about you. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking in any version of the app.

No advertising, ever
No analytics or tracking SDKs
No in-app purchases
No location, camera or contacts
Signing in is entirely optional
You can delete your account in the app

What the app stores on your device

Whether or not you sign in, Beamfall saves your progress using the operating system’s standard local storage. This never leaves your phone:

Levels clearedWhich of the 100 levels you have completed
Star ratingsHow many diamonds you earned on each level
Best tapsYour fewest-taps record per level
Hints usedHow many of your three hints you have spent
SettingsWhether haptics and colour-blind shapes are switched on

Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently.

If you choose to join the leaderboard

Beamfall has an optional global leaderboard. It is off until you deliberately sign in, and the game is complete without it. If you never sign in, nothing in this section applies to you.

Signing in uses Sign in with Apple. We never see or handle a password. Apple lets you hide your real email address behind its private relay, and that choice is entirely yours.

Once signed in, the following is stored on our behalf so the leaderboard can work:

Account identifierThe anonymous user id Apple issues for this app, plus the email address Apple returns — which may be one of Apple’s private relay addresses
Display nameThe name shown on the leaderboard. You choose it and can change it
Level resultsFor each level you clear: the level number, taps used, diamonds earned, and whether a hint was used

That is the entire list. We do not collect your contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or any behavioural or analytics data.

Who processes it

Leaderboard data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database provider acting as our processor, and with Apple for the sign-in itself. Nobody else receives it. We do not sell, rent or share it, and it is never used for advertising.

What other players can see

A public leaderboard is public. Other signed-in players can see your display name and your scores — levels cleared, diamonds, total taps and perfect routes. They cannot see your email address or your account identifier. If you would rather not be identifiable, choose a display name that is not your real name.

Deleting your account

Open the leaderboard in the app and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your display name and every score attached to it. Your progress on your own device is kept, and you can carry on playing offline. You may also email us and we will do it for you.

Third parties

Beamfall contains no advertising, analytics, social or crash-reporting SDKs. No third party receives any information about you for advertising or profiling.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (Europe), PIPEDA (Canada), India's DPDP Act and the CCPA (California) give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data an organisation holds about you, and to object to its processing.

For this website and for Beamfall, we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or erase. The only data Beamfall creates is the progress file on your own device, which is entirely under your control and is removed when you delete the app. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time and we will.

Children

Beamfall is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website either.

Changes to this policy

If our products change in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes to how a shipped app handles data would also be described in that app's release notes.
