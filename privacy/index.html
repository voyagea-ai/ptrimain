This website

ptriinnovation.com is a static site. It has no user accounts, no database and no server-side logic of its own. In practice that means:

  • No cookies are set and nothing is written to your browser's local storage.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts run. We do not measure your visit, and we cannot tell you apart from any other visitor.
  • Our contact forms do not submit anywhere. They assemble a message and hand it to your own email application. Nothing reaches us until you press send yourself, and it arrives as an ordinary email.

Two things that are honest to disclose

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore makes a request to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request, as it would for any site using the service.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested file) for security and reliability, as essentially all web hosting does. We do not analyse these logs to build any profile of you.

If you email us, we hold that email — and whatever you chose to put in it — in our mailbox so we can reply to you.

Beamfall for iOS

App Store privacy label — Data Not Collected

Beamfall collects no data whatsoever.

No accounts. No sign-in. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. No crash reporting. The app makes no network requests of any kind and is fully playable in aeroplane mode.

No account or sign-in
No analytics SDKs
No advertising SDKs
No in-app purchases
No location, camera or contacts
No network requests at all

What the app stores on your device

Beamfall saves your progress using the operating system's standard local app storage. That is the only data the app creates, and it never leaves your phone:

Levels clearedWhich of the 100 levels you have completed
Star ratingsHow many diamonds you earned on each level
Best tapsYour fewest-taps record per level
Haptics settingWhether vibration feedback is switched on

This data is not uploaded, not backed up to us — we operate no servers for this app — not shared and not sold. Deleting the app deletes it permanently. Because there is no account, progress does not transfer between devices.

Third parties

Beamfall contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social or crash-reporting SDKs. No third party receives any information about you through this app.

Our other products

PTRI Innovation also builds products that genuinely do need accounts and server-side data to work — Voyagea, Dwello and Ptrionix among them. Those are separate services with their own data practices, and each is governed by its own privacy policy rather than this page.

If you are looking for the policy covering one of those products and cannot find it, write to us and we will point you to the right document.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (Europe), PIPEDA (Canada), India's DPDP Act and the CCPA (California) give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data an organisation holds about you, and to object to its processing.

For this website and for Beamfall, we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or erase. The only data Beamfall creates is the progress file on your own device, which is entirely under your control and is removed when you delete the app.

If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time and we will.

Children

Beamfall is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website either.

Changes to this policy

If our products change in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes to how a shipped app handles data would also be described in that app's release notes.
