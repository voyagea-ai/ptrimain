01  ·  LIVE

Voyagea

voyagea.travel

Tell it where you're going and how you like to travel. Voyagea returns a complete day-by-day plan — routes, budgets, food, and the small hyperlocal things a good friend would tell you. Every trip you take earns a stamp in your V-Passport.

  • Itineraries generated in seconds, not evenings of tabs
  • Maps, day budgets and hyperlocal recommendations
  • V-Passport — a digital stamp for every place you land
  • iOS app and web sharing one account and one library

02  ·  PROTOTYPE

Dwello

by AirTrace · Windsor, Canada

A little desk robot that senses your room with radar — not a camera — and actually reacts to you. Alexa waits for you to speak. Dwello already knows you walked in.

  • Presence, movement and position — even when you sit still
  • No lens, no video: nothing about your room leaves your room
  • A face that blinks, sleeps, wakes and answers back
  • Walk in and the lights come on; leave and everything powers down

03  ·  LAUNCHING 14.09.2026

Ptrionix

ptrionix.com

Every project. One blueprint. Ptrionix is the operating system for construction and interior design firms — auto-generate BOQs, track materials, finance and site logs, and run every project from one AI-powered plan instead of six spreadsheets.

  • Bills of quantity generated from the drawing set
  • Materials tracked from purchase order to site delivery
  • Budget, spend and margin in a single project ledger
  • Daily site logs, vendors and team assignments in one place

Beamfall

iPhone & Android · Puzzle

Fire a beam across a grid, rotate mirrors and prisms to bend it, and light every target in its own colour. 100 levels, every tenth one a boss run — and every level proven solvable by computer before it shipped.

  • No advertising and nothing to buy — ever
  • No account, no sign-in, no data collected
  • Works fully offline, in aeroplane mode
