Meet
Dwello.

A little desk robot that senses your room with radar — not a camera — and actually reacts to you.

Join early access → See what it sees

A PTRI Innovation product · built as AirTrace · Windsor, Canada

Click Dwello · it reacts

Expressions

Alexa waits for you to speak.Dwello already knows you walked in.

01  /  What it does

Five things, done properly.

No app-first gimmicks. Dwello is useful the moment it's plugged in and the Wi-Fi is off.

Senses the room

Radar picks up presence, movement and position — even when you're sitting still.

No camera. Ever.

No lens, no video, no images. Nothing about your room leaves your room.

Has a face

Blinks, looks around, gets sleepy when you go, wakes up when you're back.

Talks back

Greets you, answers you, and tells you to stand up once in a while.

Runs your room

Walk in and the lights come on. Leave and everything powers down.

Feels touch, too — tap it, pet it, poke it, and it reacts.
