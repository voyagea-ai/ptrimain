01  /  What it does

A charger on the map isn't a charger that works.

Other maps tell you where a charger is. ChargeBuddy tells you what riders found when they got there.

01

Every charger, one map

No switching between network apps. Open it and the map is already on the chargers near you, anywhere in India.

02

Only chargers that fit

Tell it once what you drive — scooter, auto or car — and it hides the chargers you can't use.

03

Status from riders

Working or down, from the people who were just there. Connectors, speed and price on every charger.

04

One-tap check-in

Charged, charging, couldn't charge or waiting. One tap helps the next rider, and earns XP and badges.

05

On the car screen

Nearby chargers on Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, with directions handed straight to your maps app.

06

Start as a guest

No sign-up to start. Sign in with Apple or Google later and your check-ins and saved chargers come with you.

02  /  Build notes

Designed, built and run by PTRI.

ChargeBuddy is our own product, from the data pipeline to the store listings. It runs on phones, in the car and on the web — with live check-ins, push alerts and an admin desk behind it.
