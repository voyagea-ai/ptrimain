01  /  The problem

Six spreadsheets and a WhatsApp group.

That's how most builders run a project today. The quantities live in one file, the money in another, and the site knows something neither of them does.

01

BOQ

Bills of quantity generated from the drawing set and priced against your own rate library — not retyped from scratch on every job.

02

Materials

Every item tracked from purchase order to site delivery, so nobody discovers a shortfall on the morning of the pour.

03

Finance

Budget, committed spend, invoiced and margin — per project and across the book, in one ledger the office and the owner both trust.

04

Site logs

Daily logs from the people actually on site: progress, labour, weather, blockers — timestamped and attached to the project.

05

Vendors

Suppliers, subcontractors, rates and history in one record, so the next quote starts from what the last job really cost.

06

Teams

Who is on which site, on which day, doing what — assignment and accountability without another group chat.

02  /  Why us

We already build for the trades.

AK Builds & Plumbing is a PTRI site. Dwello is PTRI hardware. Ptrionix is what happens when a studio that ships software spends enough time around renovation crews to learn where the day actually goes.
